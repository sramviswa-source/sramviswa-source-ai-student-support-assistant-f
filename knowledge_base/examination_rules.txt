DOCUMENT: End-Semester Examination Rules and Conduct
CODE: REG-2024-EXAM-02
LAST REVISED: August 2024

1. HALL TICKET & IDENTITY CARD MANDATE
No candidate shall be admitted to the examination hall without producing a valid Hall Ticket issued by the Controller of Examinations (COE) and the official College Student Identity Card. If an ID card is lost, a temporary duplicate pass must be obtained from the Dean of Student Affairs.

2. ENTRY TIMINGS & GRACE PERIOD
Students must be seated in their allotted examination rooms at least 15 minutes before the commencement of the exam.
- A maximum grace period of 10 minutes from the start time may be permitted by the Chief Superintendent under exceptional circumstances.
- No candidate will be admitted into the exam hall after 10 minutes from the scheduled start time.
- Candidates cannot leave the examination hall during the first 60 minutes or during the final 10 minutes of the examination.

3. PROHIBITED ITEMS AND GADGETS
The following items are strictly prohibited inside the examination premises:
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, fitness trackers, Bluetooth devices, earphones, programmable calculators, electronic organizers.
- Any printed material, handwritten notes, scribble pads, or unauthorized logarithmic/data books.
Possession of any prohibited device, whether switched on or off, will be treated as an act of malpractice.

4. PERMISSIBLE ITEMS
- Non-programmable scientific calculators (models fx-82, fx-991MS, fx-991ES or approved equivalents).
- Standard stationery: blue/black pens, pencils, erasers, sharpeners, scales, transparent pouches.
- Official data books and codebooks supplied directly by the COE office.

5. MALPRACTICE REGULATIONS & DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
Any candidate caught cheating, copying, communicating with other students, possessing chits or unauthorized material, or attempting impersonation will be immediately suspended from that examination session.
- The case will be referred to the Examination Malpractice Inquiry Committee (EMIC).
- Penalties range from cancellation of all written examinations in the current semester to debarment for one or two subsequent academic years, depending on the severity of the offense.
